Our Story

25 years of turning houses into homes. One beautiful piece at a time.

Our Mission

Crafting Spaces That Inspire

Founded in 1999 in Salem, Tamil Nadu, STACKLY HOME began as a small family workshop with a single belief: every home deserves furniture built with soul, not just function.

Over 25 years, we've grown into a nationally recognized premium furniture brand, but our core values remain unchanged — uncompromising craftsmanship, sustainable materials, and a genuine commitment to making every customer's home a sanctuary.

Today, we serve over 20,000 happy homeowners across India, with a curated collection of 500+ handpicked furniture pieces for every room and every style.

Unmatched Craftsmanship

Every joint, every stitch, every finish is inspected by our master craftsmen before it leaves our workshop.

Sustainable by Design

FSC-certified wood, recycled textiles, and water-based finishes — we build for the future, not just for today.
