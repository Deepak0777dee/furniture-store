Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to common questions about ordering, delivery, and our products.

How long does delivery take?

+

Standard delivery takes 7–14 business days. Express delivery (3–5 days) is available for most items in select cities. You'll receive real-time tracking updates via SMS and email.

What is your return policy?

+

We offer a 30-day hassle-free return policy. If you're not completely satisfied, we'll pick up the item and issue a full refund or exchange — no questions asked.

Does delivery include assembly?

+

Yes! Our white-glove delivery service includes professional in-home assembly at no extra cost. Our team will place furniture exactly where you want it and remove all packaging.

Can I customize fabric or finish?

+

Absolutely! Most of our sofas, beds, and chairs are available in multiple fabric, color, and finish options. Visit our store or contact us for custom order options.

What warranty do you offer?

+

All STACKLY HOME products come with a 5-year structural warranty and a 1-year warranty on upholstery and finishes. We also offer extended warranty plans for additional coverage.
